Why do order files keep breaking?

Your WMS expects perfect data, but the sources of that data are chaotic. Manual intervention is the hidden cost of scaling.

API Encoding Errors

Shopify or Amazon APIs often spit out weird characters (like %20 or –) in descriptions. Your WMS rejects these instantly, stalling your floor operations.

"Fat-Finger" Human Errors

Customers often mistype SKUs (e.g., "HOOD-XL" vs "HOODIE-XL"). If it doesn't match your master list exactly, labels won't print and lines stop moving.
