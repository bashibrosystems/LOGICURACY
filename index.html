Why do order files keep breaking?

Your WMS expects perfect data, but the sources of that data are chaotic.

API Encoding Errors

Shopify or Amazon APIs often spit out weird characters (like %20 or –) in descriptions. Your WMS rejects these instantly, throwing the whole order into the Exception Queue.

"Fat-Finger" Human Errors

Customers often mistype SKUs (e.g., "HOOD-XL" vs "HOODIE-XL"). If it doesn't match your master list exactly, labels won't print and your line operators stop moving.

Manager's Reality

"When an order file is broken, you have pickers standing in the aisles at $25/hour while an operations manager spends 45 minutes doing manual corrections. That's not just a typo—it's a profit leak."
